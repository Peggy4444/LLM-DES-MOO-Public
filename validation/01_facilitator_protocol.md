# Facilitator protocol (for Pegah)

**Do not share this file with participants.** Print `02_participant_packet.md`
for them.

## Before the session — checklist

- [ ] Streamlit app running locally (not the public URL — safer)
- [ ] Case A pre-loaded; walk the wizard end-to-end once yourself
- [ ] Screen + audio recording tool tested (QuickTime / OBS / Zoom)
- [ ] Signed consent form printed x1 per participant
- [ ] Participant packet printed x1 per participant (with rating sheets)
- [ ] Two backup screenshots per stage saved to disk in case the API fails:
      DES parameters table, Mermaid diagram, Pareto CSV + scatter,
      SCORE frequency table, SCORE NL rationale, adapter KPI report
- [ ] A blank notebook for your field notes
- [ ] Water, snacks, offer a break at the 45-min mark

## Recommendation on format

- **Run participants separately** (1 hour each, back-to-back or on
  different days). Avoids one anchoring the other.
- **90 min per participant** is the target. If the participant is time-
  constrained, cut Block 5 (free exploration) first, then compress
  Block 6 (interview) — never cut the before/after tasks (Block 4).

---

## Session agenda (90 min)

### Block 1 — Consent + background (0:00–0:10)

- Hand them the participant packet.
- Walk through consent (recording, anonymized quotes, right to stop).
- They fill in the background form while you set up screen recording.
- Start recording once they sign.

### Block 2 — Framework walkthrough (0:10–0:25)

You drive the Streamlit app. Keep it high-level; do not evangelize.

Show, in order:
1. The five-stage pipeline diagram (from the paper or a slide).
2. Case A: load the event log, show the parsed line topology.
3. Advance through S2 → S5 quickly, naming each LLM agent as it runs.
4. Land back on the welcome / summary page.

Say: *"You'll now use the app yourself for three specific tasks. Please
think aloud as you work — say what you see, what confuses you, what you'd
do differently."*

### Block 3 — Warm-up task T0 (0:25–0:30)

Ask them to load Case A themselves and reach the point where the DES is
generated. Purpose: confirm they can navigate the UI. Note where they
hesitate.

### Block 4 — Before/after LLM interpretation (0:30–1:05) — **critical block**

For each of the three tasks below, follow this exact sequence:

1. Show the **raw artifact** (table or plot, no LLM text).
2. Ask them to **interpret it aloud** for 2–3 minutes. Do not prompt or
   correct. Write down key phrases verbatim.
3. Show the **LLM's interpretation** of the same artifact.
4. Ask the four **agreement questions** (in packet §4).
5. Have them fill the **paired-interpretation rating row** in the packet.

#### T1 — DES topology (10 min)

- **Raw:** the machine-parameter table (cycle times, MTBF, MTTR, power)
  extracted from Case A. No diagram, no LLM output.
- **Ask:** *"Sketch or describe the line for me. What do you think this
  line looks like? What would you check before trusting these numbers?"*
- **Then reveal:** the Mermaid diagram generated by the visualizer agent.
- **Agreement questions.**

#### T2 — Pareto front (12 min)

- **Raw:** the Pareto CSV opened as a table + a plain scatter plot
  (TH vs WIP), no annotations, no LLM commentary, no knee marker.
- **Ask:** *"Which point on this front would you recommend to a plant
  manager? Why? Is there anything about this front that concerns you?"*
- **Then reveal:** the LLM-labeled knee / low-WIP / high-TH selection
  with its explanatory text.
- **Agreement questions.**

#### T3 — Bottleneck analysis (13 min)

- **Raw:** the SCORE frequency table (per-machine improvement frequencies
  across the non-dominated solutions). No ranking narrative, no NL text.
- **Ask:** *"Which machine would you invest in first, and why? What would
  you tell your maintenance team?"*
- **Then reveal:** the LLM's ranked alleviation list with natural-language
  rationale.
- **Agreement questions.**

### Block 5 — Free exploration (1:05–1:20)

Give them 15 min to click through the Streamlit UI on their own. Ask them
to try one thing that interests them (e.g., adapt the model, change
objectives). Say nothing unless they ask. Note every point of confusion.

### Block 6 — Semi-structured interview (1:20–1:35)

Ask, in this order (record verbatim answers):

1. What is the **single most useful output** the framework produced today?
2. What is the **single output you would not trust** yet, and what would
   have to change?
3. Under your company's IT and data-governance policy, could you send a
   production log to OpenAI? What would need to change (on-prem, EU
   hosting, redaction, contract)?
4. Are the human-in-the-loop checkpoints in the right places? Any missing?
   Any redundant?
5. Compared to your current workflow (FACTS / Plant Simulation / Arena /
   manual), where does this framework help most, and where does it help
   least?
6. If you had a colleague who is a mechanical engineer with no simulation
   background, could they use this tool? What would they need first?
7. Would you use this tool on a real production line at your company in
   the next 12 months? Under what conditions?

### Block 7 — Overall rating instruments (1:35–1:45)

Hand them the SUS + Trust + Barrier item sheet in the packet. Let them
fill it silently.

### Block 8 — Wrap-up (1:45–1:50)

- Thank them.
- Explain: you will send them a 1-page written summary of what you heard
  within 48h and ask them to confirm you represented their view correctly
  (member-checking). Get their preferred email.
- Ask if you may quote them by role (e.g., "a simulation engineer at a
  European truck OEM") in the paper. Get this in writing.
- Stop recording.

## What to collect and store per participant

Put everything in `validation/data/PXX/` where PXX is P01, P02.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `consent.pdf` | Signed |
| `background.pdf` | Filled form |
| `ratings.pdf` | Filled artifact rating sheets |
| `interpretation_pre.txt` | Verbatim of their T1/T2/T3 raw-data interpretation (transcribed from recording) |
| `interpretation_post.txt` | Verbatim of their agreement / disagreement with LLM output |
| `interview.txt` | Verbatim of Block 6 answers |
| `sus_trust_barrier.pdf` | Filled overall instrument |
| `field_notes.md` | Your notes: hesitations, errors, quotes to remember |
| `recording.mp4` | Screen + audio |
| `member_check_response.txt` | Their sign-off on your summary, received later |

## What to compute after the session

- Per artifact: median and range of the five Likert dimensions across
  both participants (this becomes Table X in the paper).
- SUS score per participant (0–100). Report both; do not average N=2.
- Trust score per participant.
- Barrier reduction: single item.
- **Agreement metric per task (T1, T2, T3):** did the LLM's
  interpretation contain the same primary conclusion the expert reached?
  Score: full agreement / partial agreement / disagreement, with a
  one-sentence justification each. This is the headline number.
- Thematic coding of open answers into the three §7.4 themes: trust and
  auditability, decision value, adoption barriers. Add a fourth theme
  only if two independent themes emerge.
